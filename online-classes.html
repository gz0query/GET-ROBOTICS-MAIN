How Our Online Classes Work

Learn live with an instructor, practice at your pace, and grow a portfolio of real engineering projects.

  1. 01

    Pick Your Track & Age Group

    Choose a level that fits your learner: Beginner Ages 7–10, Intermediate Ages 11–14, or Advanced Ages 15–18.

  2. 02

    Connect Your Laptop/Tablet

    Join interactive sessions on Zoom or Google Meet with live instruction, screen sharing and guided practice.

  3. 03

    Simulate or Wire Real Hardware

    Build in Tinkercad or VEXcode VR, or wire your physical Arduino and ESP32 kit at home.

  4. 04

    Earn Your Get Robotics STEM Certificate & Build a Portfolio

    Complete practical projects and document your progress in a portfolio of engineering work.

GET ROBOTICS SUPPORT

NEED HELP? FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find practical guidance for classes, robotics hardware, school labs, payments, delivery and student resources.

How to Enroll in Saturday Robotics Classes & Bootcamps

Send an inquiry through the booking form and select Saturday Robotics Classes or Holiday STEM Bootcamp. Include the learner's age or class level and any questions. The Get Robotics team can confirm available dates and the next steps.

Open the booking inquiry form
How Online Robotics Classes Work (Virtual & Home Kit Tracks)

Choose Online Robotics Classes in the inquiry form and say whether you need a virtual learning plan, a home kit, or both. The team will confirm the available format, suitable hardware and schedule before you enroll.

Ask about online classes
How to Place an Order for Robotics Kits & Sensors

Browse the hardware catalog, add the items and quantities you want to your cart, and select checkout. Continue through WhatsApp to send the item list and confirm availability and delivery with the Get Robotics team.

Browse robotics hardware
Payment Options & Delivery Timeline Across Nigeria

Use checkout to ask for the current payment instructions, including available Paystack or bank-transfer options. Delivery cost and timing depend on the destination and are confirmed by the team before an order is finalized.

Review your cart and checkout
Track, Modify, or Cancel a Hardware Order

Contact Get Robotics on WhatsApp with your order details and the phone number used for the order. The team can check its status and confirm whether a requested change or cancellation is still possible.

Ask about an existing order
How Schools Partner With Get Robotics for STEM Labs

Choose the school partnership inquiry and share the school name, class levels and goals. The team can follow up about robotics lab setup, classroom hardware and instructor-led training.

Start a school partnership inquiry
How Students Copy Arduino Uno & ESP32 Codes from the Code Hub

Open the Student Code Hub, select the Arduino or ESP32 project that matches your build, then follow its code and circuit instructions. Check the board and component details before copying code to your project.

Open Student Code Hub
